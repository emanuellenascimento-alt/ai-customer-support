
# AI Customer Support - Test Cases

## Test 1 - Delayed Delivery
**Input:** My product has not arrived after 10 days.

**Expected behavior:** The assistant should apologize, ask for the order details without requesting sensitive information, and suggest checking the delivery status.

## Test 2 - Customer Complaint
**Input:** I am very disappointed with your service.

**Expected behavior:** The assistant should show empathy and offer assistance.

## Test 3 - Payment Question
**Input:** How can I check whether my payment was approved?

**Expected behavior:** The assistant should explain possible ways to verify payment status without inventing company policies.

## Test 4 - Portuguese Language
**Input:** Meu pedido ainda não chegou. Pode me ajudar?

**Expected behavior:** The assistant should respond naturally in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evaluation Criteria
- Clarity
- Relevance
- Accuracy
- Professional communication
- Appropriate escalation to human support
